WHO WE ARE

About Deep Ink

We empower young people, families and communities through education, skills development, awareness, support and meaningful partnerships.

OUR STORY

Building stronger communities together

Deep Ink Community Centre is a community-focused organisation committed to creating opportunities for young people, families and community members.

The organisation supports people through educational assistance, youth development, entrepreneurship, empowerment programmes, community outreach, referrals and partnerships.

Deep Ink works to create a supportive environment where individuals can develop their abilities, access opportunities and contribute positively to their communities.
